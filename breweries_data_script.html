First Coast Brewery Data Timothy Gibbons/Jacksonville Business Journal 2025-10-28

Overview

As of October 28, 2025, there are 348 breweries with current licenses in the state of Florida, dating back to Yuengling Brewing Company Of Tampa Inc, which received its license on April 14, 1999.

This data updates automatically when the script is run, but it can be manually downloaded here.

On the First Coast1, there are 38 breweries with current licenses. The first, Bold City Brewery Llc, was issued its license on an original date of September 08, 2008.

An embeddable version of this chart can be found here.

Growth

The number of breweries across the state has been steadily rising. The most recent brewery to be licensed in Florida is Grand Scheme Brewing in Gainesville, which was permitted on October 27, 2025.

On the First Coast, the most recent permit was issued to Southern Swells Brewing Company in Jacksonville Beach on an effective date of October 24, 2025.

Sales

The state collects data (for taxation purposes) on direct sales by breweries – generally speaking, these are sales made in a taproom. (Unfortunately, overall sales numbers, showing sales through the brewer-wholesaler-retailer system, are not available.)

This data must be downloaded manually; the most recent information was pulled October 28, 2025. The data, which is split between breweries and brewpubs, can be found here.

Breweries across the state obviously saw those sales collapse during the height of the Covid-19 pandemic. Right after the pandemic, the First Coast rebounded more than the state as a whole.

Embeddable versions of the Florida chart is here and of the First Coast chart is here

The brewery in Florida that has sold the most beer directly to consumers in 2023 is Anheuser Busch, which has sold 82,826.74 gallons.

Among First Coast craft breweries — excluding Budweiser — Wicked Barley’s taproom moved the largest amount of beer, selling 37,752.86 gallons directly to consumers.

In the latest 12 months of data – from September 2024 to August 2025 – the 36 breweries and brewpubs in the area that reported sales moved 290,952.3 gallons of beer.
